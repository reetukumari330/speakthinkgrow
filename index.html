What a 15-minute lesson looks like

  1. Remi asks a question

    A warm, everyday question opens the lesson — no right or wrong answer, just a starting point.

  2. Your child speaks first

    They record or say their answer out loud before seeing anyone else's — building the habit of speaking before doubting.

  3. A thinking game follows

    A short puzzle or story choice turns the topic into a bit of gentle reasoning practice.

  4. Remi celebrates the win

    The lesson ends with a sticker, a streak, or a note home — so trying again tomorrow feels easy.
